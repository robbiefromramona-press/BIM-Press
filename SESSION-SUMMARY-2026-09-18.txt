BIM-Press.com homepage rebuild: session summary, 2026-09-18
=============================================================

STATUS: Built and tested locally. NOT committed or pushed yet (waiting on your OK).

WHAT CHANGED
------------
The homepage was rebuilt to match the reference mockups in _reference/
(landscape = desktop, portrait = tablet/phone).

1. Header (stays pinned to the top on scroll)
   - Hamburger (3-bar) icon, BIM-PRESS logo + "BIM - at the Press of a button!",
     search box, SIGN IN, JOIN.
   - Wide screens also show an inline nav (Apps & Tools / Games / Videos / Contact /
     TotalStationTech). RESOURCES and ABOUT from the mockup were left out because
     there are no pages for them yet.
   - Phones: search folds into a magnifier icon that opens a search row.

2. Hamburger dropdown menu: Apps, Games & Training, Videos, Contact, TotalStationTech.com
   - Apps and Games & Training jump to their sections, and each lists its apps
     as direct links.
   - Videos opens a popup with videos/bim_press_intro.mp4 (no autoplay; closing pauses it).
   - Contact opens the existing contact email form.

3. Hero: "BIM MADE PRACTICAL", tagline, EXPLORE APPS & TOOLS button, LEARN/BUILD/SHARE/GROW
   icons, PEOPLE / TOOLS / KNOWLEDGE / A BETTER / BUILT WORLD list, and the handwritten
   "Build Smarter Together". The photo is your _reference/bim-press-homepage-hero-only.png,
   compressed to WebP (193 KB desktop, 79 KB phone) plus a JPG fallback.

4. Apps and Tools: QRPoint, CoordXY, PDF-Inspect, P-DXF cards with LEARN MORE buttons.
   VIEW ALL TOOLS shows a second row with CSV Converter and Field Calculator.

5. Games and Training: Stakeout and TriBrach cards. EXPLORE TRAINING goes to the
   BIM-Press YouTube channel.

6. Footer: tagline "Better models. Brighter people. A more connected built world.",
   YouTube icon, "At the press of a button.", then a row with "(c) 2026 Any-Press LLC",
   Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Disclaimer, email/contact, Report an Error,
   and TotalStationTech.com.

7. New legal pages: generic small-business placeholder text. They say so in an HTML
   comment at the top of each file, and [State] in terms.html still needs filling in.

8. JOIN opens the old Founding Member form (same email-app behavior as before, now in a popup).
   SIGN IN shows a "Member sign-in is coming soon" note. No account system, Netlify signup
   form, or Stripe logic was built or touched.

All icons are drawn in code (SVG), so they stay sharp at any size and no extra PNGs are needed.
